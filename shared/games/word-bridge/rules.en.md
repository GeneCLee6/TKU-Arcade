## What is Word Bridge?

A word game for partners. 25 words lie on a 5×5 grid. A secret key says which words are yours. Your caller sees the key and gives one-word clues that bridge to as many of your words as possible; you pick the words you think they mean. Play two partnerships against each other, or two or three of you together against the clock.

The team game plays like the board game Codenames (Vlaada Chvátil, Czech Games Edition), and the co-op mode borrows the idea of a two-sided key from its two-player edition; the name, the words, the wording and the look here are our own. Word Bridge is a working title.

## The team game

- Two partnerships: **North–South** and **East–West**, as at a bridge table. Each has one **caller**; everyone else picks.
- Before the deal, pick sides in the game's own setup: join a side, become your side's caller, or press Random sides. Each side needs a caller and at least one partner.
- Only the two callers see the key: 9 words for the side that starts, 8 for the other, 7 **bystanders** and 1 **trap**. The side that starts is random.

## A turn

1. **The call**: your caller types one word and a number, for example "sailing 2". The number says how many words the clue is meant for; "any" is allowed too.
2. **The picks**: tap a word, then turn it. You can pick up to the number plus one.
   - Your own word: it turns your colour and you may go on.
   - A bystander or the other side's word: it turns, and your turn ends.
   - The trap: your side loses at once.
3. After at least one pick you may end your turn yourselves.

The first side to turn all its words wins, even if the other side turned the last of them.

## Clues

- One word only: no spaces, no digits, at most 12 characters.
- Not a word still face down on the grid, and not part of one either way round: with 火雞 on the grid, both 雞 and 火雞肉 are refused. Width and case do not matter. Once a word is turned, it may be used.
- Say nothing else: no hints by voice, no faces. The partners may talk among themselves as much as they like.

## Co-op

- For 2 or 3 players (or any number, chosen as the mode). Two players hold one **side** of the key each; with three, the third picks on every clue.
- Each side shows 9 targets and 3 traps. Some targets are on both sides; a trap on one side may be a target on the other. 15 words to find in all.
- The key holders take turns giving clues. Everyone else picks, judged by the side of whoever gave the clue: a target lets you go on with no limit, a bystander ends the turn (and is marked for that side), a trap loses the game.
- A side with no targets left hands the clue back to the other.
- You have 10 clues by default. When they run out comes the **last stand**: no more clues, each key holder turns the partner's words from memory, and one miss loses.
- Find all 15 to win.

## Options

Set by the host in the waiting room.

- **Mode**: Auto (co-op for 2–3, teams from 4), Two teams (needs 4), or Co-op.
- **Minutes per clue and per picks (0: no clock)**: 0 by default, up to 5. The clock runs once for the clue and again for the picks; when it runs out, the turn passes.
- **Co-op: clues before the last stand**: 6 to 15, 10 by default. Fewer is harder.

## When someone is away

The host can skip a caller who is due to give a clue, or the pickers: the turn passes. In the last stand, skipping a key holder ends the game.

## The words

Every word is our own choice, in Chinese only for now: things with more than one meaning that link to each other, with some bridge, campus and Hong Kong film words.

## Players

2 to 10 players. A game takes about 20 minutes.
