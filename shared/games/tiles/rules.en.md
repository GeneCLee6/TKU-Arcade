## What is Clubroom Tiles?

A number-tile game for the club room. Lay your tiles on the table in runs and groups, rework the sets already there to fit more of yours, and be the first to empty your rack. Clubroom Tiles is a working title.

It plays like the tile game Rummikub (Kodkod); the name, the bridge-suit tiles, the wording and the look here are our own.

## The tiles

- 106 tiles: the numbers 1 to 13 in four suits (spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs), two of each, and 2 **wildcards** with a star.
- Each player gets 14 tiles in their rack. The rest are the pool, face down.
- Sort your rack **By number** or **By suit**; only you see your rack.

## Sets

- A **group** is 3 or 4 tiles of the same number, each in a different suit.
- A **run** is 3 or more numbers in a row in one suit. 1 never comes after 13: 12-13-1 is not a run.
- A wildcard stands in for any tile a set is missing: 5, wildcard, 7 of hearts is a run; 12, 13 and a wildcard is 11-12-13.
- A set is just the tiles in it: the site puts them in order for you.

## Your first play

Your first play must be worth **30 points** or more, made only from tiles in your rack, in new sets. A tile counts its number; a wildcard counts the number it stands for. Until then you may not touch the sets already on the table.

## A turn

1. Lay tiles from your rack: start new sets, or add to sets on the table.
2. Rework the table if you like: split sets, join them, move tiles from one set to another. Every tile that was on the table stays on the table.
3. A wildcard on the table can be taken back by putting the tile it stands for in its place; you must use it again in a set the same turn.
4. Press **Done**. It works when every set on the table is a group or a run and you added at least one tile from your rack.
5. Can't or won't play? Press **Draw**: the table goes back to how it was when your turn began, and you take one tile from the pool.

On your turn, tap tiles to select them (from your rack, and once you have made your first play, from the table), then tap the **+** at the end of a set, or **New set**. On a computer you can drag tiles too. **Undo** takes back your last move, **Reset turn** all of them. Everyone sees the table change as you move tiles.

## The turn clock

The host can give each turn 60, 90 or 120 seconds, or no clock. When time runs out, the table goes back and you draw a tile, as if you had pressed Draw.

## The end of a round

- The first player to empty their rack wins the round.
- When the pool is empty, a player who cannot play passes. Once every player has passed in a row, the round ends and the lowest rack wins.
- Scoring: every other player loses what is left in their rack (a tile its number, a wildcard 30), and the winner gains the total. Tied lowest racks share the gain, rounded down.

## Options

Set by the host in the waiting room.

- **Turn clock**: 60 s, 90 s (the default), 120 s, or Off.
- **Rounds**: 1 to 5, 1 by default. Scores add up over the rounds; between rounds the score stays up until someone presses Next round, or 45 seconds pass.

## When someone is away

If the player on turn is away, the host can skip them: their turn ends as if they had drawn.

## Players

2 to 4 players. A round takes about 30 minutes.
