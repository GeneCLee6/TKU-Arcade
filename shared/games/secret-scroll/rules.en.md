## What is Secret Scroll?

A quick card game of bluffing and deduction in a jianghu world. Sixteen characters are after a lost martial-arts scroll. You hold one card; on your turn you draw a second and play one of the two. Knock the others out of the round, or hold the highest card when the deck runs dry, and you win the round and a renown token. Collect enough renown to win the game.

The draw-one, play-one idea with sixteen numbered cards comes from Seiji Kanai's card game Love Letter; the characters, card names and texts, the look and the jianghu theme here are our own. Secret Scroll is a working title.

## The cards

Each card has a value from 1 to 8. The deck has 16 cards.

- **1 Constable** (5 cards): pick a player and name any card but the Constable. If they hold it, they are out.
- **2 Spy** (2): look at a player's card. Only you see it.
- **3 Duelist** (2): compare cards with a player in secret. The lower card is out; a tie does nothing.
- **4 Monk** (2): until your next turn, no card can pick you.
- **5 Drunkard** (2): a player, you included, throws their card away and draws a new one.
- **6 Mask Maker** (1): trade cards with a player.
- **7 Strategist** (1): no effect, but you must play it if you also hold the Drunkard or the Mask Maker.
- **8 Secret Scroll** (1): if you ever throw it away, for any reason, you are out.

## Setting up a round

1. The deck is shuffled and one card is set aside face down, unseen. With 2 players, 3 more are set aside face up for everyone to see.
2. Everyone gets one card.
3. The first round starts with a random player; later rounds start with the last round's winner.

## Your turn

1. Draw a card: you now hold two.
2. Play one of them face up and do what it says. Cards that pick a player cannot pick someone who is out or shielded by the Monk; if nobody can be picked, the card is played with no effect. Only the Drunkard can pick you yourself.
3. Played cards stay face up in front of you, so everyone can see what has gone.
4. The turn passes to the next player still in.

When the Drunkard makes someone draw and the deck is empty, they take the card set aside face down.

## Out of the round

A player who is out shows their card and sits out the rest of the round.

## Who wins the round

- When only one player is left, they win the round.
- When the deck is empty after a turn, everyone still in shows their card and the highest wins. A tie goes to whoever has played the highest total; a tie on that too is shared.

The winner (or each winner of a tie) takes a renown token. The next round deals itself 30 seconds after a round ends, or as soon as anyone presses Next round.

## Winning the game

The first to collect enough renown wins: 7 tokens with 2 players, 5 with 3, 4 with 4, 3 with 5 or 6. A short game needs 4, 3, 3 and 2.

## Options

Set by the host in the waiting room.

- **Renown to win**: Standard or Short (see above).
- **Add 2 Beggars (5–6 players)**: two extra cards of value 0. The Beggar: look at the top card of the deck. With Beggars in, the Constable can also name the Beggar. Meant for big tables, where the 16-card deck runs out fast; off by default.

## When someone is away

If a player is away on their turn, the host can skip them: they fold, show their cards, and are out of that round.

## Players

2 to 6 players; best with 2 to 4. A game takes about 20 minutes.
