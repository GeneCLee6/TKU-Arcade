## What is Clue Clash?

A word game you all play together. Each card, one of you guesses a mystery word that everyone else can see. Everyone else writes one clue, without showing the others. Clues that clash, because two of you wrote the same thing, cancel out before the guesser sees them. So be helpful, but not too obvious.

It plays like the party game Just One (Ludovic Roudy and Bruno Sautter, Repos Production); the name, the words, the wording and the look here are our own. Clue Clash is a working title.

## One card

1. **The word**: the guesser takes turns round the table. Everyone else sees the word and its category; the guesser sees a question mark. Anyone who does not know the word can press New word, once per card.
2. **The clues**: everyone but the guesser types one word. With 3 players, each writes two different clues. You may change yours until all are in.
3. **The clash**: the site strikes out clues that are the same once spaces, punctuation, width, case and an English plural are ignored. Then the clue-givers see every clue and cross out any that mean the same, such as a plural or the same root; a clue is out when half the clue-givers (rounded up) cross it. Everyone presses Done.
4. **The guess**: the guesser sees only the clues left and has one guess, or passes.
5. **The reveal**: everyone sees the word, the guess and all the clues. The guesser moves on to the next card.

## Scoring

- **Right**: 1 point. The other names for the same thing listed with the word count too.
- **Pass**: this card is lost.
- **Wrong**: this card is lost, and so is the next one. On the last card, a point is lost instead.
- If the guess was right in all but spelling, or another name for the same thing, the clue-givers can press Count it as right.

13 cards make a game, so the best score is 13.

## Clues

- One word only: no spaces, at most 12 characters.
- Not the word, and not part of it either way round: for 珍珠奶茶, both 奶茶 and 珍奶 are refused.
- No clue by voice, faces or chat: chat is closed until the reveal. The guesser should not look at anyone else's screen.

## Options

Set by the host in the waiting room.

- **Clock (90 s to write, 60 s to guess)**: on by default; crossing out gets 45 seconds. When writing runs out, whoever has not sent a clue gives none this card; when guessing runs out, it counts as a pass.

## When someone is away

The host can skip a clue-giver who has not written or pressed Done, and the guesser: a skipped guess counts as a pass.

## The words

Every word is our own choice, in Chinese only for now: Taiwanese food, campus life, bridge and board games, Hong Kong films and more.

## Players

3 to 7 players. A game takes about 20 minutes.
