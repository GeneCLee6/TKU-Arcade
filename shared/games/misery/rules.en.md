## What is Misery Line?

A party game about knowing just how bad things can get. Every card is a miserable event with a **misery index** from 1 to 100: 1 is a small annoyance, 100 is rock bottom. Each player keeps a **line** of cards in front of them, from the least to the most miserable. On your turn a new event comes up with its index hidden, and you say where in your own line it belongs. Argue about it on your own voice call or at the same table; the site deals, judges and reveals.

It plays like the party game Shit Happens, published by Goliath; the name, cards, indexes, wording and look here are our own. "Misery Line" is a working title.

## Your line

- Everyone starts with a few face-up cards (3 by default), each showing its event and its index, sorted from the lowest index to the highest.
- Everyone's line is open for the whole table to see.
- Two cards may share an index: a card with the same index is right on either side of its twin.

## One card

1. **Draw**: the player whose turn it is draws a new event. Everyone sees what happened and its category; the index stays hidden. Feel free to read it out dramatically on the call.
2. **Place**: that player picks a gap in their own line (between two cards, before the first or after the last). If the index falls between the cards on either side, it is right.
3. **Right**: the card goes into their line and its index is shown.
4. **Wrong**: the table only learns that it was wrong; the index stays hidden. With **Steals** on, the next player in seat order may place it in **their own** line; if they miss too, the next one tries, each player once. With two players, the other player steals.
5. Once someone gets it right, or everyone has tried, the index is shown. A card nobody got is thrown away.
6. After the reveal, the next player in seat order presses "Draw a card" and it is their turn. Turns go round in seat order, whoever stole the last card.

Each wrong try shows which two indexes it went between, so the players stealing after it can use that.

## The end

The first player whose line reaches the target (10 cards by default) wins, and the game ends there; stealing the last card counts. If the deck runs out before anyone gets there, the longest line wins, and players can tie. The results list how many cards are in each line.

## Options

The host sets them in the waiting room.

- **Cards to win**: 8, 10 or 12 cards, 10 by default.
- **Starting cards**: 1 to 5 cards, 3 by default.
- **Steals**: on by default. Off, a wrong card is thrown away at once.
- **Time to guess**: no clock, 30 s or 60 s, 60 s by default. Every try gets its own time; when it runs out, the try counts as a miss and the next player may steal.

## When someone is away

If the player who should place the card is away, the host can skip them: the try counts as a miss and the next player may steal. If the player who should draw the next card is away, skipping them draws it for them, and the turn is theirs as usual.

## The cards

Every event and index was written for this site. There are nearly 400 cards in 12 categories, in Chinese only for now.

## Players

2 to 10 players. A game takes about 25 minutes.
