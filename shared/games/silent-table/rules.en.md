## What is Silent Table?

A card game you win together, without saying a word. Everyone is dealt a poker hand, nobody may talk, and the table has to work out, from rank chips alone, whose hand is strongest and whose is weakest.

## The goal

Put the hands in the right order. **Clear 3 levels in a row and the whole table wins. Fail 3 levels in all and the whole table loses.** A failed level sets the run back to zero, but failures never go away. The host can change both numbers.

## One level

1. Everyone gets two hole cards that only they can see.
2. Five shared cards are turned over in three rounds: three, then one, then one.
3. In each round, everybody takes one rank chip. Chip 1 says "I think I will end up weakest", the highest chip says "I think I will end up strongest". It is about the final seven cards, so a good guess now may be wrong later.
4. Once everyone holds a chip and has pressed **Ready**, the next cards are turned over and all the chips go back.
5. After the third round, the hands are shown. If the chips of the third round match the real order of the hands, the level is cleared. The chips of the first two rounds are only hints.

## Taking a chip

- Take any chip lying on the table. If you held one, it goes back.
- Or take the chip someone else holds. They then have none and must take another. This is how you say "no, I am stronger than that".
- You have **5 moves per round**. Swapping a chip or taking one from a player is a move. Taking a chip from the table when your hands are empty is free, so you can never be left without one.
- Any chip moving cancels everyone's Ready: Ready means "I accept the chips as they are now". You can still move after pressing it.

## No talking

Chat is closed during the rounds, and on a call the same goes for you. Between levels, when the cards are up, you may talk it over. Then everyone presses **Next level** for a new deal.

## Ties

Hands that are exactly equal may hold their chips in either order, as long as the group sits in the right place.

## Hands, weakest to strongest

Your best five out of the seven cards count. In a tie of category, the higher cards decide.

1. High card
2. Pair
3. Two pair
4. Three of a kind
5. Straight
6. Flush
7. Full house
8. Four of a kind
9. Straight flush

## Options

The host picks these in the waiting room.

- **Levels in a row to win**: 1 to 7, 3 by default.
- **Failed levels that end the game**: 1 to 5, 3 by default.
- **Chips can be taken from other players**: on by default. Turned off, only the table's chips can be taken.
- **Spectators see all hole cards**: off by default. Without it spectators see only the shared cards and the chips until the reveal.
- **Time for each round**: no clock by default, or 60, 90 or 120 seconds. When the time is up, whoever holds no chip gets the lowest one left on the table, and the round moves on as it stands.
- **Table theme**: a plain table, a diving team or a climbing team. It changes how the table looks, not the rules.

## Players

3 to 6 players. If someone drops out, the host can decide for them: they take the lowest chip left and say ready.
