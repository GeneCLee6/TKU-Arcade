## What is Wasabi Dumplings?

A bluffing and bidding game at a dumpling stall. Everyone has four dumplings: three plain ones and one stuffed with wasabi. Hide them in your steamer, then bid how many dumplings you can eat without biting any wasabi. Eat them all and you win a gold chopstick; bite wasabi and you lose a dumpling. Two gold chopsticks win the game.

The hide-and-bid idea comes from Hervé Marly's game Skull; the dumplings, the stall, the wording and the look here are our own. Wasabi Dumplings is a working title.

## Your dumplings

- Each player starts with 3 plain dumplings and 1 wasabi dumpling.
- Only you know which of yours is which. The others only see how many you have.

## One round

1. **Hide one**: everyone hides one dumpling in their steamer, face down, all at the same time.
2. **Add or bid**: in turn, starting with the round's first player, each player either hides another dumpling on top of their steamer, or starts the bidding. With no dumplings left in hand, you must bid.
3. **Bid**: a bid says how many dumplings you can eat, from 1 up to every dumpling on the table. Going round, each player raises the bid or says I'm full. Once you are full you are out of this bidding. The bidding ends when everyone else is full, or as soon as someone bids every dumpling on the table.
4. **Eat**: the highest bidder eats that many dumplings, one at a time, each turned face up for everyone to see. They must eat their whole steamer first, top down; then from any steamers they choose, always from the top.

## Winning a bid

Eat as many as you bid without any wasabi, and you win a gold chopstick.

## Biting wasabi

As soon as you bite wasabi, you stop eating and lose a dumpling for good:

- **Someone else's wasabi**: its owner takes one of your dumplings, face down, without seeing which. Nobody but you learns what you lost.
- **Your own wasabi**: you choose which dumpling to lose.

Lose your last dumpling and you are out of the game.

## The next round

Everyone takes back their dumplings and a new round starts, a few seconds after the last one or as soon as anyone presses Next round. The last bidder goes first; if they are out of the game, the owner of the wasabi they bit goes first.

## Winning the game

The first player with 2 gold chopsticks wins. If everyone else runs out of dumplings, the last player with dumplings wins.

## Options

Set by the host in the waiting room.

- **Gold chopsticks to win**: 1 to 3, 2 by default.

## When someone is away

The host can skip a player who is away. The site then makes the mildest move for them: hides a plain dumpling (the wasabi only if nothing else is left), says I'm full in the bidding, or bids 1 if they have nothing to hide. If they are eating, it eats their own steamer first and then the first steamer in seat order; if they must take a dumpling, it takes the first one.

## Players

3 to 6 players. A game takes about 20 minutes.
