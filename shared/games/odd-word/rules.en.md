## What is Odd Word Out?

A party game about words that are almost the same. Everyone gets a word, but one or two of you get a slightly different one, and nobody is told which word they have. Take turns describing your word, listen closely, and work out who the odd ones out are. You talk on your own voice call or face to face; the site only deals the words, picks who speaks first and shows everything at the reveal. There are no votes and no scores.

It plays like the well-known party game 誰是臥底 ("who is the undercover"); the name, the word pairs, the wording and the look here are our own. Odd Word Out is a working title.

## Your word

- Each round the site draws a pair of similar words (for example two night-market snacks). Most players get one; the **odd ones out** get the other.
- How many are odd ones out: 1 for 3 to 6 players, 2 for 7 or more. The host can choose 1, 2 or 3 instead, but the odd ones out are always fewer than half the table (2 needs 5 players, 3 needs 7).
- Everyone sees how many odd ones out there are this round, never who.
- With the **blank** on, one of the odd ones out gets no word at all and sees "No word". The blank knows they are odd; everyone else only knows their own word.
- Friends sitting together can glance at your phone: tap your word, or Cover my word, to cover it.

## One round

1. **Words**: everyone reads their own word. The site shows a random speaking order, from a random first player, in seat order or in reverse.
2. **Describe**: on your call, go round in that order. Each player says one thing about their word without saying it. Go round as many times as you like, and argue about who sounds off.
3. **Reveal**: the first speaker runs the round. When the table is done, they press Reveal: everyone's word, both words and who the odd ones out were are shown to all.
4. The first speaker presses Next round, and a new pair is dealt with a new speaking order.

## New words

Before the reveal the first speaker can press New words, if someone already knows the pair or the words do not work for your table. The new pair goes to a new set of odd ones out; the speaking order stays.

## The end

The game ends after the last round, 8 by default. After any reveal the first speaker can also press End game. Nobody wins: the results list how many times each player had the odd word.

## Options

Set by the host in the waiting room.

- **Odd ones out**: Auto (1 for 3 to 6, 2 for 7 or more), or 1, 2 or 3, capped to fewer than half the table.
- **A blank (no word)**: off by default.
- **Rounds**: 1 to 30, 8 by default.

## When someone is away

Only the first speaker can move the game on. If they are away, the host can skip them: before the reveal the next speaker in the order takes over the round; after the reveal the next round starts.

## Chat

Chat is closed until the reveal, so nobody types their word, and opens at the reveal.

## The words

Every pair is our own writing, in Chinese only for now.

## Players

3 to 12 players. A game takes about 20 minutes.
