## What is Tamsui Traders?

A trading game set on the Tamsui river harbour in the tea years. Take goods off the wharf, buy stalls, shops and trading houses, and let each one you own make the next one cheaper. Big patrons come calling once your business covers what they want. The first trader to reach 15 points ends the round; the most points win.

It plays like the card game Splendor (Space Cowboys); the theme, cards, patrons, numbers, wording and look here are our own. Tamsui Traders is a working title.

## The goods

Five goods: **tea**, **camphor**, **indigo**, **sugar** and **pepper**, each with its own colour and shape. **Gold** is wild: it pays for any good.

- On the wharf: 4 of each good with 2 players, 5 with 3, 7 with 4. There are always 5 gold.
- You may hold at most **10 tokens**. Go over and you put tokens of your choice back until you have 10.

## On your turn

Do exactly one of these:

1. **Take three different goods**, one of each. When fewer than three goods are left on the wharf, take one of each that is left.
2. **Take two of one good**, only from a pile with **4 or more** in it.
3. **Reserve a card**: take a face-up card into your hand, or the top card of a deck face down (only you see it), and take **1 gold** if any is left. You may hold at most **3 reserved cards**.
4. **Buy a card**, face up in the market or one you reserved. Pay its cost in goods; gold covers anything you are short.

## Cards

- The market has three tiers, **stalls**, **shops** and **trading houses**, with 4 cards face up in each. A bought or reserved card is replaced from its deck.
- Every card you own is a lasting **discount of one** in its good: with two tea cards, every later cost in tea is 2 less. Discounts can make a card free.
- Stalls are cheap and score 0 or 1 point; shops score 1 to 3; trading houses cost the most and score 3 to 5.

## Patrons

- Players + 1 patrons are on display. Each wants a number of cards of certain goods (for example 4 tea and 4 camphor).
- At the end of your turn, if your cards cover a patron's wants, that patron **comes on their own**, worth **3 points**. Only cards count, never tokens.
- At most one patron comes in a turn: the first one on display that you qualify for.

## The end

- When a trader reaches **15 points**, the round is played to its end, so that everyone has had as many turns.
- The most points win. A tie goes to whoever bought **fewer cards**; still tied, the place is shared.
- In the rare game where nobody can move for a whole round, it stops there; a game also stops after 60 rounds.

## When someone is away

The host can skip an absent player's turn. If they are holding more than 10 tokens at that moment, the room puts tokens back for them first.

## On the screen

Tap goods on the wharf, or a card or a deck, and confirm in the bar at the bottom. A button only lights up for a move the rules allow.

## Players

2 to 4 players. A game takes about 30 minutes.
