## What is Bluff Reader?

A party game about who bluffs best. Each round everyone sees the same obscure term, but only one player gets its real meaning; the others make one up on the spot that sounds just as true. The judge listens to every reading and has to find the one telling the truth. You read face to face or over a voice call; the site deals the cards, keeps time, takes the votes and keeps score.

The idea of one player reading the real meaning while the rest bluff and a judge finds the honest one comes from the Taiwanese board game 9upper (TIME2PLAY GAMES); the terms, wording, look, the mole, the heckle stickers and the scoring here are our own.

## Roles

- **Judge**: taken in seat order, once each a game (the host can make it twice). Everyone knows who the judge is. The judge sees the term only.
- **Honest reader**: drawn in secret from the others, gets the **real meaning** and reads it out as it is.
- **Mole**: with 4 players or more, one more is drawn in secret. The mole bluffs too, and also gets **3 secret words** to work into their reading. The site does not check whether they did; everyone listens.
- **Bluffers**: everyone else. They see the term and its three categories, and make up a meaning that sounds true.

Every term comes with **three categories** (say, "an old official post / a kind of bird / a traditional pastry"). Everyone sees them, the judge too. The real meaning belongs to one of them, but only the honest reader knows which. Bluffers may pick any of the three; the reveal shows the true one.

Each player knows only their own role.

## One round

1. **Cards**: everyone reads their own card, for 15 seconds or until the judge presses Start reading. If a neighbour might peek, cover your card.
2. **Reading**: everyone but the judge reads in the random order the site gives, one at a time, 60 seconds each (the host can change it). The reader or the judge can end a reading early.
3. **Vote**: 60 seconds. The judge votes once for the honest reader. Everyone else (the honest reader and the mole too) may give **1 heckle sticker** to the reader they found worst, never themselves, or keep it; the judge gives none. Before the reveal you can see who has decided, not whom they picked.
4. **Reveal**: once the judge has voted and everyone has decided (or time runs out; anyone undecided keeps their sticker), everything is shown: the honest reader, the mole, the secret words, the real meaning, the judge's vote and every sticker. The round is scored.
5. The judge presses Next round, and the next seat judges.

## Scoring

| What happened | Points |
| --- | --- |
| The judge picks the honest reader | Judge +2, honest reader +2 |
| The judge picks a bluffer or the mole | That player +3 |
| A sticker on the honest reader | Its giver −1 |
| A sticker on the mole | Its giver +2 |
| A sticker on a plain bluffer | 0 |
| Nobody stuck a sticker on the mole this round | Mole +2 |
| The judge did not vote (time ran out, or away) | Nobody scores for the vote; stickers still count |

The game ends once everyone has judged. The highest score wins; equal scores share the place.

## Options

Set by the host in the waiting room.

- **Everyone judges twice**: twice as many rounds as players.
- **Seconds to read**: 20 to 180 seconds, 60 by default.
- **A mole with secret words (4 players or more)**: off, there is no mole and no secret words.
- **Show three categories, one of them true**: on by default. Off, everyone sees the term only.
- **Countdowns**: off, there is no countdown for cards, reading or the vote; the buttons move the game on.

## When someone is away

- A judge away while the cards are read: the host can skip them and start the reading.
- A reader away while reading: the next reader goes on.
- Anyone undecided away at the vote keeps their sticker; a judge away has no vote.
- A judge away at the reveal: the host can start the next round for them.

## Chat

Chat is closed while the cards are read, during the reading and the vote (one "I have the real one" would end the game), and opens at the reveal.

## The terms

Every term is real but little known, and every explanation is in our own words. The terms are in Chinese only for now.

## Players

3 to 9 players. A game takes about 30 minutes.
